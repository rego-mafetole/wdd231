Strive2Learn Siteplan

Strive2Learn

Site Name

Strive2Learn

I chose to create a tutoring service website called Strive2Learn. This name reflects how learning requires personal effort, strong will and an active intent.

Optional domain availability: LearnLaunch.net

Site Purpose

The aim/purpose of this website is to provide personalised academic instruction and learning support tailored for individual student needs. The website offers a structured place to study after hours, helping students improve their focus, master difficult concepts, and successfully prepare for upcoming tests and exams. It will focus more on core subjects like math and science, test preparation, or skill development for students outside of normal school hours.

Scenarios

  • "Where can I find the pricing packages and book last-minute sessions before my final exams?"
  • "Do you offer virtual or physical meetups with the tutors? "
